# Digital Caribbean Consortium Project Registry

Welcome to the **Digital Caribbean Consortium Project Registry**!  
This repository serves as the central hub for identifying, documenting, vetting, and incubating digital infrastructure projects essential for building a sovereign, innovative Caribbean digital region.

---

## 1. Project Focus & Problem Space

Our focus is on foundational digital infrastructure critical for Caribbean sovereignty, innovation, and regional interoperability.  
Projects typically address challenges in:

- **Technical Infrastructure:** Data centers, cloud providers, hosting, storage, network resilience, and availability.
- **Enabling Systems:** Payment gateways, cross-border banking, sovereign identity, security frameworks, regulatory tech, and interoperability.
- **Software Innovation Layer:** Platforms, open-source tools, developer enablement, regional standards, open APIs.

Problem spaces include gaps in digital resilience, cross-country collaboration, regional sovereignty, fragmentation, and barriers to Caribbean-wide digital growth.

---

## 2. Intake Process

We welcome proposals for projects that fit our focus:

1. **Submit Proposal:** Initiate a project by opening a new issue via our [project proposal template](./.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/project-proposal.md).
2. **Documentation:** Provide background, problem definition, stakeholders, technical requirements, and anticipated impact.
3. **Community Discussion:** Consortium members and the wider community review, comment, and support project proposals.
4. **Evaluation:** The governance council vets proposals for strategic fit and feasibility.

Accepted projects are catalogued in this registry and may be transitioned to dedicated repositories for development.

---

## 3. Governance & Solution Adoption

- **Stewardship:** Proposals are managed by a consortium-appointed Governance Council, ensuring alignment with regional priorities.
- **Transparency:** All project documentation, vetting notes, and decision rationale are publicly available.
- **Criteria:** Solutions must address identified gaps, be technically and regionally viable, and demonstrate potential for long-term impact.
- **Adoption:** Upon approval, projects receive formal support, resources, and guidance for development and deployment.

---

## 4. Commercialization

- **Guidelines:** Approved projects may explore commercialization opportunities, with the Consortium facilitating partnerships and pathways to market where aligned with regional interests.
- **Models:** Open source, managed services, and public-private partnerships are all supported, provided they comply with consortium values and regional regulations.
- **Value:** Commercialization must preserve public good, promote regional technology independence, and foster sustainable ecosystems.

---

## 5. Incubation

- **Incubator Track:** Selected projects enter an incubation phase, receiving mentorship, technical support, and introductions to funding and regional testbeds.
- **Milestones:** Incubation includes goal-setting, validation, proof of concept, and regular reviews.
- **Transition:** Successful projects may graduate to independent repositories and enter deployment pilots.
